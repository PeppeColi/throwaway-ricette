# Glossary

The domain vocabulary of `ricette`. Use these terms in code, tests and documentation.

- **Item**: the name of a food or product the cook wants to buy, such as "milk".
- **Shopping list**: the ordered list of items to buy, kept in `ricette-list.json`.
- **Shopping list entry**: one line of the shopping list, holding one item; duplicates are allowed.
- **Quantity**: how much of an item is needed, as a number (for example "2"); not yet stored by the list.
- **Unit**: the measure a quantity is counted in, such as grams, millilitres or pieces; not yet stored by the list.
- **Recipe**: a set of instructions for cooking a dish, with the ingredients it needs.
- **Ingredient**: an item a recipe needs, with the quantity and unit required.
